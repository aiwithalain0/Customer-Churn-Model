# Brag Plan: CustomerIQ

## What is this app?
CustomerIQ is an enterprise customer churn prediction and Explainable AI (XAI) analytics platform that identifies high-risk accounts before they leave and delivers transparent SHAP factor attribution using XGBoost.

## The angle
Enterprise AI product launch with an authoritative, data-first posture: moving beyond black-box churn scores to exact, actionable causality. It demonstrates real numbers from the project (7,043 customer accounts, 3,063 flagged at-risk, 26.5% churn baseline) and showcases individual SHAP attribution so teams know exactly why an account is leaving before taking action.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
A dramatic, high-contrast opener: "Every customer leaves clues. Most companies only notice after they're gone." A warning radar sweeps across high-risk account signals before resolving into the live CustomerIQ intelligence canvas.

## Key moments (the middle)
- **4 Real-Time KPI Cards**: Total Customers (7,043), At-Risk Accounts (3,063), Churn Rate (26.5%), Predicted Retention (56.7%) popping in sequentially.
- **Model Status Live Tag**: XGBoost & SHAP Explainer Online with real-time inference.
- **Explainable AI (SHAP) Factor Attribution**: Account #7590-VHVEG showing 84.2% High Risk with exact directional drivers (`Month-to-month Contract`, `Tenure Cohort`, `Fiber Optic`).
- **Proactive Retention Action**: Retention recommendation triggered with 1-click Export Report.

## Outro / punchline
"CustomerIQ — Smarter Insights. Stronger Relationships."
FastAPI • XGBoost • SHAP Explainability.

## User flow worth showing
Entry (Dashboard Overview & At-Risk Alert) → Key Action (Selecting high-risk customer #7590-VHVEG & inspecting individual SHAP force drivers) → Result (Simulated retention offer reduces risk + instant Export Report).
